This paper is a two-part empirical study that begins with the selection of a basket of diversified assets upon which we apply mean-variance methodology and diversification constraints to construct and optimise a portfolio satisfying the investor’s return requirements. We then proceed to predict and manage risk through volatility analysis using Generalised Autoregressive Heteroskedasticity (GARCH) framework to model risk over the investment horizon. Our resulting portfolio is made up of 14 assets, diversified across asset classes, regions, currencies and sectors. We train a Markov Switching GARCH model on the portfolio returns and find a satisfactory level of accuracy, supporting the hypothesis that the ability to account for regime shifts in markets produces a better fit than other GARCH models.
